Debate Desk

Two agents argue about SOL from the same evidence. A third judges. Fixed rules decide whether the ruling may trade, and every decision lands on Solana devnet as a receipt in the same transaction as its trade. The reasoning trail cannot be edited after the fact.

How to check every word on this page
  1. Each card's receipt hash is committed by program GkCqpfzoMVSCMw4muiKAXoo4kcHFRiYjjAHBQjmhMwmU into the desk's on-chain log Ahw9K3YcVcaK....
  2. The receipt names the hashes of every evidence item and every model transcript. Those files are in the repo under data/.
  3. Run the verifier. It re-hashes everything, proves bull and bear saw the identical evidence, replays the risk rules to the same action and size, and replays the on-chain head.
